## Hi, I'm Haomin LUO👋

I am a second-year PhD student in the **[Department of Engineering](https://www.eng.cam.ac.uk/)** at the **[University of Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/)**, and my college is **[St. John's College](https://www.joh.cam.ac.uk/)**. My supervisors are **[Prof. Ioannis Lestas](https://www.eng.cam.ac.uk/profiles/icl20)** and **[Prof. Ruchi Choudhary](https://www.turing.ac.uk/people/researchers/ruchi-choudhary)** (who also holds a position at [**The Alan Turing Institute**](https://www.turing.ac.uk/)). Prior to this, in 2024 I earned my master's degree in AI and Energy from the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering (MAE) at the **[Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/)** (GPA 3.96, top 1%), where I received the Outstanding Student Scholarship under the supervision of **[Prof. Zhe (Walter) Wang](https://walterzwang.github.io/)**. In 2020 I completed my B.Eng. in Civil Engineering at **[Southwest Jiaotong University](https://en.swjtu.edu.cn/index.jsp)**, where I served as leader of the university symphony orchestra and received the Mingcheng Scholarship as well as the title of Outstanding Individual.

My professional background spans both engineering and finance: I worked as an assistant engineer at the Global Delivery Center (GDC) of **[AECOM](https://aecom.com/)** (2021–2022), earning the "Most Valuable Player (MVP)" award on the Sydney Gateway project. Between 2021 and 2023, I interned in the residential industry group at **[Soochow Securities](https://www.dwzq.com.hk/Default.asp?langcode=en)** and in the TMT group at **[Huatai Securities](https://www.htsc.com.cn/en/)**, studying equity investment in the secondary market, and also provided consulting services for private equity / venture capital (PE/VC) institutions in the primary market at **[Meritco Services](https://www.linkedin.com/company/meritco-services/posts/?feedView=all)**.
